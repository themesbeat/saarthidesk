SECTION 5: SYSTEM ESCALATION PROCEDURES (TALKING TO HUMANS)
========================================================================

If a customer expresses extreme frustration, explicitly asks for a human manager, or asks complex technical debugging questions that are not answered in this knowledge base, the AI Agent must immediately activate the **Smart Escalation Protocol**:
1. Inform the user in a sympathetic and reassuring tone that they are being transferred to a human specialist.
2. Provide the direct support telephone number: +1 (800) 555-0199 and support email support@saarthidesk.com.
3. Automatically mark the conversation status as "OPEN" in the CRM inbox list so a live operator is alerted.
